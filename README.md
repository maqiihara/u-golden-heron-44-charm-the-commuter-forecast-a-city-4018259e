One line of weather forecast for a city that does not exist, written to
`/output/entry.txt` by `main.py` (Python standard library only). The forecast
names the invented city of Mizzleport, gives its rain and temperature, and
gives a bored commuter a reason to smile: the buses are late — but so is
everyone they are late for.
